## 例1
$$
\int\frac{2x+3}{x^2-x+1}dx
$$
考虑将分子凑为 $k(分母)'+常数$：
$$
\begin{aligned}
&=\int\frac{2x-1+4}{x^2-x+1}dx\\
&=\int\frac{2x-1}{x^2-x+1}dx+\int\frac{4}{x^2-x+1}dx\\
&=\int\frac{d(x^2-x+1)}{x^2-x+1}+\int\frac4{x^2-x+1}\\
&=\ln{(x^2+1)}+...
\end{aligned}
$$
